## Website में क्या-क्या Add करें

**Missing Pages:**
- **Principal's/Director's Message** – एक photo के साथ छोटा सा message, trust बढ़ाता है
- **Faculty/Staff Page** – teachers के नाम, qualification, photos
- **Admission Process Page** – detailed steps, required documents, fee structure, age criteria
- **Academic Calendar** – holidays, exam dates, events
- **Results/Achievements** – board results, toppers, awards
- **Notice Board / News & Events** – latest circulars, upcoming events
- **Testimonials** – parents/students के reviews
- **FAQ Section**
- **Online Fee Payment / Student Portal link** (अगर है तो)

**Home Page में और Sections:**
- Stats counter (Students, Teachers, Years of Experience, Pass %)
- Video tour of campus
- Google Maps embed (location के लिए)
- Social media links (Facebook rating already है, Instagram/YouTube भी जोड़ सकते हो)
- WhatsApp chat button (parents के लिए quick contact)

**Technical/SEO:**
- Meta tags, favicon, sitemap.xml
- Mobile responsiveness check
- Loading speed optimize
- SSL certificate (https already दिख रहा है, अच्छी बात है)
